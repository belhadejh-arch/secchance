---
name: Specialist consultation visibility
description: Records the user's chosen approval gate for public professional consultations.
---

Publish professional consultations to beneficiaries only after administration approves the specialist. Keep signup submissions private while they are under review.

**Why:** The user chose publication after approval rather than immediately upon signup.

**How to apply:** Keep pending providers and their consultations out of public listings and booking flows; publish after an administrator approves the professional account.
